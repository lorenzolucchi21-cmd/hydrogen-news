Each story shows the picture supplied by the publisher's feed when there is one; otherwise it shows an openly licensed illustrative photo matching the topic (a stock photo, not a picture of the event itself), with its credit. Stories from RWE's core markets (Germany, the Netherlands, the UK and Denmark) and from the rest of Europe are ranked first; stories from outside Europe rank lower, except e-SAF and e-fuels.
