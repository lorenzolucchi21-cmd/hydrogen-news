About these insights

This is a focused, manually researched watchlist, not a complete sector directory. Company roles and examples refer to specific activities, including planned projects; they do not describe every news story. Company coverage is based on name matches in headlines and feed summaries. Story tags and progress or setback signals use keywords and may be incorrect; open the original story to check. Similar reports are grouped where possible. Coverage is limited to the archive and selected sources.

Industry background: U.S. Energy Information Administration. Sources for each company's classification are shown when selected.
